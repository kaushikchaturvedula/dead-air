# DEAD AIR — code audit, 2026-08-21

**Scope:** the whole repo at commit `d98ae33` — plant, agent, scripts, Makefile,
docs, `docker-compose.yml`, `plant/alloy/config.alloy`.

**Method.** Six failure-mode lenses swept the tree independently (silent
failures, unbounded waits, model-judgement-where-code-should-decide, dangling
references, local-state dependence, plus two whole-area sweeps). Every candidate
finding was then re-checked against the source by an independent adversarial
pass whose default verdict was *false positive*; the buckets below are that
pass's corrected buckets, not the finder's claims. Three findings were
downgraded on verification and two upgraded.

**Read-only.** Nothing in the plant was executed or mutated during the audit —
no agent run, no chaos injection, no `make` target, no gcloud call. A six-case
evaluation was running concurrently and had to stay uncontaminated. The handful
of things that *were* executed (all isolated: `signatures.py` replayed
standalone, `alloy validate` in a `--network none` container, a wire-schema dump,
a `bash` `set -e` repro) are listed explicitly in "What I have not verified".

**Known incompleteness:** two verification passes died on transport errors, so
two findings carry the finder's bucket rather than a verified one. Several
findings are reasoned from source and explicitly not reproduced — B7 and B8
among them. The gap list at the end is deliberately long and is part of the
result, not a disclaimer.

**Result:** 43 raw candidates, deduped to **30** — 9 demo-breaking, 17
judging-embarrassments, 4 cosmetic — plus a short list of one-liners. Within
each bucket they are ordered by how likely they are to actually bite.
